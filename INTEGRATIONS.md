# Open-source integrations

- **No AI Slop** — editorial checklist used for concise interface copy and portfolio documentation. MIT: https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop
- **tldraw** — interactive warehouse-map canvas. Production deployment may require a tldraw license key: https://github.com/tldraw/tldraw
- **OpenAI Whisper** — planned private transcription service; the public demo uses browser speech recognition as a credential-free fallback: https://github.com/openai/whisper
- **OpenSEO** — post-deployment SEO audit target. The app includes indexable metadata, robots, and sitemap routes: https://github.com/every-app/open-seo

The repositories are integrated by capability; their full applications are not copied into StockFlow.
